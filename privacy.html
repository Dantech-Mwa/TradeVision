Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2025

TradeVision Pro ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our web‑based platform.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information: When you sign in with Google or email, we receive your email address and display name from the authentication provider (Firebase). We do not see or store your password.

Usage Data: We may collect anonymised usage data (e.g., page views, features used) to improve the platform. No personally identifiable information is included.

2. How We Use Your Information

• To provide and maintain the platform
• To personalise your experience (e.g., saved layouts, indicators)
• To communicate with you (e.g., support requests)
• To monitor and analyse usage patterns for improvement

3. Data Storage & Security

Your authentication data is stored securely in Firebase (Google Cloud infrastructure). Local settings and trading journals are stored in your browser’s localStorage. We use industry‑standard encryption for data in transit.

4. Third‑Party Services

We use Firebase Authentication (Google) for sign‑in, Google Analytics for anonymised traffic analysis, and Binance for market data. These services have their own privacy policies.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at mwanza@tradevisionpro.online.
