Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 13, 2025

TradeVision Pro ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website https://tradevisionpro.online (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our Service.

We prioritize user privacy and have designed the Service to minimize data collection. The Service provides advanced charting tools, over 100 technical indicators, real-time market data via WebSocket connections from cryptocurrency exchanges, drawing tools, a Pine Script editor, backtesting capabilities, watchlists, alerts, screeners, and community features for sharing trading ideas—all without requiring user registration or accounts.

Information We Collect

We collect very limited information:

  • No Personal Data Collection: We do not require or collect any personally identifiable information (e.g., names, email addresses, phone numbers, or financial details). No user accounts or registration is needed.
  • Local Browser Storage: Features such as watchlists, alerts, custom settings, themes, and saved charts are stored exclusively in your browser's localStorage or similar client-side mechanisms. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any server.
  • Usage Data (Automatic): Like most websites, our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) and basic server logs may record non-personal information such as IP addresses, browser type, referring pages, and access times for operational and security purposes. This data is anonymous and not linked to individuals.
  • Cookies and Tracking Technologies: The core Service does not set or use cookies. However, third-party services (detailed below) may use cookies or similar technologies.

Third-Party Services and Advertising

The Service is partially monetized through Google AdSense, which displays advertisements:

  • Google AdSense and its partners may use cookies, web beacons, or similar technologies to serve personalized ads based on your browsing history across sites.
  • These third-party vendors use cookies to display relevant advertisements. Google's privacy practices are governed by their own policy, available at https://policies.google.com/privacy.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page.

We do not use other analytics tools (e.g., Google Analytics) or additional tracking services beyond what is required for AdSense functionality.

Real-Time Market Data

Market data (prices, charts, and indicators) is fetched in real-time directly from third-party cryptocurrency exchanges via client-side WebSocket connections in your browser. We do not store, process, or log this data on any server.

Community Features

Any sharing of trading ideas or scripts occurs client-side or through optional external platforms. No personal data is collected or stored by us in these features.

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, rent, or share any information with third parties, except:

  • As required by law (e.g., subpoenas or legal processes);
  • To protect our rights, safety, or property;
  • With service providers (e.g., GitHub for hosting) under standard terms.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to secure the Service, including hosting on reputable platforms. However, no internet transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security for automatically collected usage data.

Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect any data from children.

Your Rights and Choices
