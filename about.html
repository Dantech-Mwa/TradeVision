About TradeVision Pro

Empowering traders with professional-grade tools — completely free, open-source, and privacy-first.

TradeVision Pro is a modern, web-based cryptocurrency charting and analysis platform designed to deliver institutional-level features to retail traders and enthusiasts worldwide. Built with performance, transparency, and user privacy at its core, it provides everything needed for technical analysis, strategy development, and market monitoring — without accounts, tracking, or paywalls.

Mission & Vision

Our mission is to democratize access to advanced trading tools that were once exclusive to professional platforms. We believe that powerful charting, real-time data, and strategy testing should be available to everyone, regardless of budget or technical expertise.

We envision a future where open-source financial tools foster innovation, education, and fair access to market intelligence.

Key Principles

  • Privacy First: No user registration, no personal data collection, no server-side tracking. All preferences (watchlists, alerts, themes) are stored locally in your browser.
  • Open Source: Fully transparent code licensed under MIT. Anyone can inspect, contribute, fork, or self-host the platform.
  • No Paywalls: All features — including advanced indicators, Pine Script editor, backtesting, and drawing tools — are 100% free forever.
  • Performance Driven: Lightweight, fast, and optimized to run smoothly even on modest devices and connections.

Features

  • Advanced interactive charts powered by lightweight, high-performance rendering
  • Over 100 built-in technical indicators and studies
  • Real-time market data streamed directly from major cryptocurrency exchanges via WebSocket
  • Full-featured Pine Script editor with syntax highlighting and execution
  • Powerful backtesting engine for strategy validation
  • Custom watchlists, price alerts, and market screener
  • Drawing tools, multiple chart layouts, and timeframes from 1 second to monthly
  • Community ideas section for sharing and discovering trading strategies
  • Dark/light theme support with auto-save preference

Open Source & Community

TradeVision Pro is proudly open source. The entire codebase is publicly available on GitHub, allowing developers, traders, and contributors to:

  • Review the code for security and transparency
  • Report issues or suggest improvements
  • Submit pull requests to add new features or indicators
  • Fork and customize for personal or commercial use under the MIT license

Monetization & Sustainability

The platform is sustained through non-intrusive advertisements served via Google AdSense. We carefully balance ad placement to maintain a clean, professional user experience while supporting ongoing development and hosting costs. No user data is shared or sold — ever.
