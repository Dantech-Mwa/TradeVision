Who We Are

Africa's leading data science consultancy for development organizations, private sector, and individuals

Bridging Data & Impact Across Sectors

Founded in 2018 by Mwanza Wambua, Danis Consulting emerged from a recognition that organizations across Africa were collecting vast amounts of data but struggling to extract meaningful insights. We serve a diverse clientele including NGOs, government agencies, private companies, and individuals seeking data expertise.

We combine advanced statistical methods with deep contextual understanding of African development and business challenges. Our team of data scientists, economists, and sector specialists work across health, education, agriculture, governance, and private sector domains.

Headquartered in Nairobi, we serve clients across 16 African countries, with particular expertise in East Africa. We understand both the technical requirements of data analysis and the practical realities of implementation across different sectors.

Our Differentiator

We specialize in both development sector analytics AND private sector business intelligence, with comprehensive training programs for individuals and organizations. This unique combination allows us to provide holistic data solutions tailored to Africa's specific context.
