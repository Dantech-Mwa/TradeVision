Who We Are

Africa's leading data science consultancy for development organizations

Bridging Data & Development Impact

Founded in 2018 by Mwanza Wambua, Danis Consulting emerged from a recognition that development organizations across Africa were collecting vast amounts of data but struggling to extract meaningful insights.

We combine advanced statistical methods with deep contextual understanding of African development challenges. Our team of data scientists, economists, and sector specialists work across health, education, agriculture, and governance sectors.

Headquartered in Nairobi, we serve clients across 16 African countries, with particular expertise in East Africa. We understand both the technical requirements of data analysis and the practical realities of development implementation.

Our Differentiator

Unlike generic data science firms, we specialize in the development sector. We understand donor reporting requirements, M&E frameworks, and the unique data challenges faced by NGOs and government agencies in Africa.

Case Studies

Real projects delivering measurable impact across Africa

Health Systems Strengthening - WHO East Africa (2022-2023)

Client: World Health Organization (WHO) Regional Office for Africa
Challenge: Improve data utilization for health decision-making across 8 East African countries

We developed an integrated health data analytics platform connecting DHIS2 systems across Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda, Ethiopia, South Sudan, Somalia, and Djibouti. The platform enabled real-time monitoring of 42 health indicators and predictive analytics for disease outbreaks.

68% Faster data reporting
42 Health indicators monitored
8 Countries integrated
$320K Project value

Impact: Reduced disease outbreak detection time from 14 days to 3 days. Informed allocation of $15M in emergency health funds. Platform adopted as regional standard.

Education Data Analytics - UNICEF Kenya (2023)

Client: UNICEF Kenya Country Office
Challenge: Address learning poverty and school dropout rates in arid and semi-arid regions

Conducted analysis of national assessment data from 3,200 schools to identify predictors of dropout and learning poverty. Developed machine learning models to identify at-risk students 6 months before dropout. Created district-level dashboards for education officials.

22% Dropout reduction
3,200 Schools analyzed
15 Counties covered
$285K Project value

Impact: Informs $8M education programming. Model adopted by Ministry of Education for national rollout. Contributed to 15% improvement in learning outcomes in target regions.

Agricultural Impact Evaluation - Gates Foundation (2023)

Client: Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation
Challenge: Evaluate impact of climate-resilient agriculture program in West Africa

Designed and implemented randomized controlled trial across Ghana, Nigeria, and Senegal to assess impact of new drought-resistant seeds and farming techniques on smallholder farmer incomes and food security.

42% Income increase
15,000 Farmers in study
3 Countries
$420K Project value

Impact: Evidence led to $8.7M program expansion. Findings published in peer-reviewed journal. Methodology adopted as standard for agricultural evaluations in region.

Governance & Accountability - USAID Kenya (2021-2024)

Client: USAID Kenya
Challenge: Measure effectiveness of governance strengthening program across 47 counties

Developed comprehensive governance scoring system tracking 15 accountability indicators. Created real-time dashboard for civil society monitoring. Conducted mixed-methods evaluation combining quantitative surveys with qualitative interviews.

35% Transparency improvement
47 Counties assessed
15 Accountability indicators
$750K Project value

Impact: Informs $25M governance programming. Dashboard used by 120 civil society organizations. Contributed to policy reforms in 8 counties.
